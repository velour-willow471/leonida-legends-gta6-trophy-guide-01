Roadmap to the Platinum

Built only from what the 55 trophy descriptions confirm. Follow the phases in order and you will never need a second full playthrough for the story content.

Phase 1 — Play the story first

  1. Start with Just Like Paradise (Gone Fishing), then clear Chapters 1–4 for Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby! and Top Of The World, Ma.
  2. Finish the named missions as they appear: Trust (Breakfast Run), Break the Bank (The Cartel Heist), Family Matters (Child's Play), DWNFALL (Headliners), Hi-Jacked (VCIA Takedown) and Snow Storm (Operation Savage Glory).
  3. Complete the finale for You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Keep a manual save before the final mission — ending details are not confirmed by the descriptions.
  4. Along the way, finish every character strand finale: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus Strangers & Freaks (8 stranger strands).

Phase 2 — Completion and collectibles

  1. Push toward A Long Road (100% Completion) using the in-game tracker as your checklist of what remains.
  2. Collectibles: Don't Touch My Deck (3 card sets), You WAiNKer! (100 locations), Positive Mental Attitude (every meditation spot), Whip It Good (100 vehicles) and On the Spot (10 Scooter Bros registrations).
  3. Activities: Cunning Stunts (250 stunts), The Shootist (gold in all Shooting Range challenges), Top Fun (drone race win everywhere), Earn Cash, Not Kudos (20 different race wins) and Master of None (try every activity type once).
  4. Money and property: Dime on the Dollar ($250,000 laundered), Treat Yo Self ($100,000 spent) and Little Boxes (all garages).
  5. Explore everything for The Goodtime State, and rob for Maximum Withdrawal, A Classic Blitz Play and Pest Control.

Phase 3 — Cleanup of one-off moments

  1. Deliberately set up the partner sequences: Hot Together and Retail Therapy — both need one continuous sequence, so plan the route first. ⚠ Missable-style: a broken sequence means a fresh attempt.
  2. Do the stealth and set-piece trophies with a save ready: Sneakthief, Catch Me If You Can, Capital Offense and In The Beginning…. ⚠ Treat these as one-chance until you confirm they can be repeated.
  3. Honest unknowns: Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess and The Truth Is Out There have vague descriptions — the guides in the list say plainly what is and is not confirmed instead of inventing steps.
  4. When the counter reaches 54 of 55, Vice Got a Price! — the platinum — is yours.
