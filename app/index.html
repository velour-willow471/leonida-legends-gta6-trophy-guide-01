Roadmap to the Platinum

There are 55 trophies in total: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. No trophy in the official list requires online play, so the whole platinum can be planned as a single-player route. The order below keeps backtracking low and puts the one choice-sensitive trophy in view early.

Stage 1 — Story first

Play the main story from Gone Fishing through the chapters to the final mission. This clears the chapter trophies, the named mission trophies such as Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory, and opens up the whole state of Leonida for everything else. Finish character strands as they appear — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each end with a final-mission trophy, and eight stranger strands complete the silver Strangers and Freaks trophy.

Stage 2 — Completion and collectibles

With the map open, work on the 100% completion gold. Sweep exploration area by area for The Goodtime State, meditate at every spot, finish three collectible card sets, discover vehicles as you travel, and use the WAiNK action at new locations instead of repeating old ones. Mix in the volume work here: 250 stunts, 50 challenges, and sampling every activity type once.

Stage 3 — Activities and skill

Clear the activity block: gold medals in all shooting range challenges, a drone race win at every Top Fun location, 20 different race wins, beating Cal in a fishing challenge, and completing one arcade game. These are repeatable, so failed attempts cost only time.

Stage 4 — Cleanup of one-off actions

Finish the situational trophies in free roam: partner wanted-level escapes, shop and vault robberies, the undetected jewelry store robbery, an armored truck robbery, one illegal operation per gang, the money totals for laundering and shop spending, all garage purchases, and ten Scooter Bros registrations. End with the platinum itself, Vice Got a Price!, which unlocks once the other 54 are done.

Missable warnings

Only one trophy in this guide is flagged missable: Actions Speak Louder, because it tracks choices and story choices are often one-time decisions. Keep rotating manual saves before major decisions so a different path can be revisited. For every other trophy, the official description reads as repeatable or cumulative, but a save before the final mission is still cheap insurance. Vague trophies such as Challenging Tank, Personal Best and The Truth Is Out There have honestly uncertain methods — their cards say so plainly rather than inventing steps.

100% checklist summary

  • Story: all chapters, the final mission, and every named mission trophy.
  • Strands: final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus 8 stranger strands in total.
  • Exploration: all areas of Leonida explored and every meditation spot used.
  • Collectibles and discovery: 3 card sets complete and 100 vehicles discovered.
  • Activities: every activity type tried, 250 stunts, 50 challenges, shooting range golds, drone race wins everywhere, 20 race wins.
  • Money and property: laundering total, shop spending total, and all garage properties bought.
  • Cleanup: partner, robbery, stealth and one-off action trophies, then the platinum.
