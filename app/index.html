Roadmap to the Platinum

Estimates from the published guides: difficulty about 4/10, around 200 hours (KeenGamer; Push Square says up to 300). One playthrough, no online trophies, free roam after the story. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep manual saves — 15 slots — at the start and end of each chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving while active.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story, and do the one thing that can be missed (60–80 hours)

    Play through the four chapters with Jason and Lucia. Story trophies pop automatically; you don't need gold medals in story missions. While you play, scan new vehicles and study new animals with the WAiNK app and binoculars — you'll need 200 base-model vehicles and 100 animal species later, and collecting them naturally saves hours.

    The missable: after the Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason's house in the morning. When she mentions meditating, follow her to the balcony and accept. That balcony session may not count as a marked meditation spot later, so do it before leaving Chapter 1 and make a manual save. It is the only currently identified potentially missable trophy-related interaction, for Positive Mental Attitude (18 meditation spots). Whether the balcony session is strictly required is still unconfirmed — do it anyway.

    Character-only questlines: Andy's strand is Lucia-only; Tyrone's and Bret's are Jason-only. Irredeemable profile: some choices can't be undone — the PowerPyx roadmap flags it, so keep a save before big decisions. Hourglass quests expire, but they don't count toward trophies.

  3. Step 3 — Finish the stranger strands and the cleanup (10–20 hours)

    Once 100% is done, check the tracker for what's left: the five side-character finals (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), eight Strangers & Freaks strands, the remaining meditation spots, and the miscellaneous combat, driving, robbery and money trophies. Then Vice Got a Price! pops with the last one.
