Roadmap to the Platinum

    Watch out for one trophy: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy flagged as possibly missable — do the balcony meditation at Jason's house during Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off for the whole run.
