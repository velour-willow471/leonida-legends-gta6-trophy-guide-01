# Leonida Legends — GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker

An unofficial, fan-made, offline trophy guide for Grand Theft Auto VI on
PlayStation 5. All 55 official trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver,
46 bronze — list frozen 2026-11-18), each with a guide written in this
guide's own words and its sources named, plus a checklist that saves in
your browser, filters by grade/kind/status, a spoiler setting that hides
story trophies until earned, a roadmap to the platinum with the 100%
completion checklist, and export/import of your progress.

## Use it

Open `app/index.html` in any browser, or serve the folder:

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```

No account, no network, no ads. Your checklist lives only in your
browser (localStorage).

## Sources

The trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5
list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles. The unlock methods are
this guide's own words, drawn from KeenGamer, PowerPyx, Push Square,
GTABase, gta6collectiblesmap and gta6database — named under each trophy
and on the Sources page. Four trophies have no confirmed unlock method
in any published guide as of 2026-11-20 (*In The Beginning…*,
*Capital Offense*, *Bad Luck, I Guess*, *The Truth Is Out There*; and
*Ten Pin*'s precise method is still being confirmed) — their entries say
so plainly instead of inventing a method.
